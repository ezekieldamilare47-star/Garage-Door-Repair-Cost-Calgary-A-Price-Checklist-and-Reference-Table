## Checklist: Before Requesting a Quote

- [ ] Identify the failed part (spring, cable, roller, track, seal, sensor or opener)
- [ ] Note the door size (standard 7/8 ft or 10-ft high)
- [ ] Check whether the door has one spring, two springs or more
- [ ] Ask for the price for the exact part, not a general range
- [ ] Confirm there is no service call, diagnostic or travel fee
- [ ] Confirm the price before work begins
- [ ] Ask about warranty on parts and labour
- [ ] Ask whether a same-day appointment is available

## Notes on the Numbers

Torsion springs are sold as a pair and priced by wire size. A Yellow (.207 wire) pair is $380, the .218 to .250 wire pair that suits most doors is $460, a Blue (.262) pair is $480, an Orange (.273) pair is $580 and a Light Blue (.283) pair is $680. A premium pair with a 5-year warranty, subject to availability, is $680. A single spring on a single-spring system is $280 with warranty, three springs are $780 and four springs are $1,040. A Torquemaster conversion is $580.

A standard pair of cables is $320 to $360, and a pair for a 10-ft high door is $390. Converting to Torquemaster is $580 and converting to an extension spring system is $680. The price can change when the door is partly stuck open, the rollers are bent or the door uses Torquemaster.

Opener installs range from $820 to $1,990, and a bare install adds $100. The SOMMER 2060 evo+ (3/4 hp, 8 ft) is $820, the SOMMER 2060 pro+ is $920 and the SOMMER 2110 evo+ (1.25 hp) is $1,020. LiftMaster options include the LM2220L chain drive at $880 for a 7 ft rail and $920 for 8 ft, the LM6580L belt drive at $1,080, $1,120 and $1,220 for 7, 8 and 10 ft, the LM6690L belt drive at $1,280, $1,320 and $1,420, and the heavy-duty LM4690L chain drive at $1,580, $1,620 and $1,720. Wall-mount jackshaft openers are $1,380 for the LM98022 and $1,990 for the heavy-duty LM98032.

Rollers for a standard 7/8 ft door are $220 for the full set. An operator bracket is $180, strut installation is $280 to $320 and hinges are $120 to $200. Track repairs are $220 for one vertical track, $280 for one horizontal track, $380 for two vertical tracks, $460 for two horizontal tracks and $580 for vertical and horizontal tracks on both sides.

Weatherstripping is $220 for a single white strip and $260 for a double white, with colored options at $260 for single and $320 for double. A bottom seal is $180 for a single, $200 for a double and $220 for an 18 ft door; with a retainer the prices are $265, $285 and $340.
